Rearranged S282 B99 2.0-beta

Public release name corrected to 2.0-beta. The earlier version numbers below
identify internal development builds. Gameplay is unchanged from the 1.23.2
candidate; the running version label and structural JSON now show 2.0-beta.

Changes from 1.23.1
- Fixed four extra Franklin cylinder assemblies inherited by rear-drive/tender
  templates when optional powered-tender parts were prepared before other layouts.
  Hide the optional source assets before cloning, retaining the native Walschaerts
  engine and all physics. This fixes the eight-found/four-expected conversion error
  on Angus, Great Northern and Erie; the strict cylinder-count test is retained.
- Structural reports also reject unexpected active Franklin assemblies on tenders.

Changes from 1.23.0
- Fixed compact 2-4-4-4-0 conversion restoring a stock Mikado when its second
  hinge is just behind the native visible bar-frame end. Hinge/driver positions
  and native constraints are retained; the empty rear bar slice is omitted.
- Powered tenders keep independent source parts, so returning to Erie after
  other layouts cannot lose its source parts when the shared Mallet cache rebuilds.
- Brought articulated headers 15 cm inward, shortened outboard cylinder entries
  and replaced the rear manifold dogleg with one rounded diagonal connection.
  Small local fitting overlaps are intentional; original style and bore retained.
- Restored just the manual structural-test button:91 arrangement/tender cases
  plus repeated returns to Erie, with current frame/cache/binding expectations.
  Power recording and resizing controls remain absent.

48 arrangements, including the new 2-4-4-4-0 articulated triplex.
It has three two-axle locomotive engines, a leading axle and no trailing axle.
Its tender is ordinary and unpowered. Existing arrangements are retained.

Choose arrangements with the Wheel Rearranger comms-radio mode. Stop, close the
regulator and let wheel slip/driving force settle before changing arrangements.
The selected arrangement is remembered with the locomotive.

Optional powered tender
Open UMM > Rearranged S282 > Powered tender - arrangement options (test).
Check the arrangements that should use the Erie powered S282 tender. This is a
global policy per arrangement, applied on new spawn, save reload or reapply.
Only Erie is enabled by default. To change an existing locomotive, highlight it
with the Wheel Rearranger radio, open UMM and press REAPPLY CURRENT ARRANGEMENT.
Uncheck and reapply to remove its tender engine. Uncheck Erie to test its loco
without a powered tender. Changes are not applied to every loaded car at once.

Works with rigid and two-engine articulated layouts and a normally coupled S282
tender behind the locomotive. Three-engine locomotive triplexes already use all
three engines, so cannot add a fourth tender engine. The unpowered 4-0-4 novelty
and unfinished giant-driver 2-2-2 prototype are excluded. Other tender types are
unsupported. Coupler separation removes the engine and restores tender visuals.

Powered tender adds pull and steam consumption; it shares the locomotive's
boiler and controls, and has no extra boiler capacity. It keeps the existing
simplified tender grip model, with host-linked RPM corrected for wheel diameter;
independent tender slip RPM and weather/sanding refinements are not included.
The locomotive's own calibration, grip, brakes and curve behaviour are retained.

Accepted wheel/cylinder/rod proportions are baked in. Old saved sizing-slider
values are ignored. Valve-gear, trailing-truck, curving, oilcup and random-spawn
preferences remain. The new arrangement is excluded from random spawns until
its own random-spawn checkbox is selected.

Structural test
UMM > Rearranged S282 > RUN STRUCTURAL TEST - two stationary S282s.
Use two stock 2-8-2 S282s in a sandbox, both stopped with regulators shut.
At least the test loco needs a regular S282 tender coupled behind it. The second
locomotive is the isolation control and must not have a powered tender active.
The test temporarily selects powered/unpowered tenders without changing saved
UMM preferences, then leaves the test locomotive in stock with an ordinary tender.
Close UMM after pressing the button so native simulation and effects can tick.
It runs 91 cases: all48 layouts with ordinary tenders plus43 compatible layouts
with powered tenders, then six direct switches checking repeated returns to Erie.
Three-engine locomotive triplexes, unpowered4-0-4 and unfinished giant2-2-2 retain
their powered-tender exclusions. JSON records each mode separately, frame pieces,
active engines, tender source/bindings, native bogie restoration and isolation.
Read stage2-runtime-checks.json in this mod folder after completion.
Small fitting overlaps and an empty final bar slice on2-4-4-4-0 are intentional.
This is a stationary structural test; listening, visible clipping, moving rods,
loaded power, curves, save/reload and distant LODs still require manual testing.
Power recording and temporary sizing sliders remain removed. Normal game/error
logging remains. No diagnostic exporter is bundled.

Manual test suggestions
- Select 2-4-4-4-0 from stock, then after another articulated layout. Confirm all
  three pairs of drivers and all three cylinder groups appear on the locomotive.
- Select Erie, switch through a rigid, a Mallet and the new triplex, then return
  to Erie. Check the powered tender returns each time. Repeat reapply and reload.
- New triplex: inspect all six drivers, both sides, rods/gear/oilcups/pipes; run
  forward/reverse at several cutoffs, on curves and at a distance.
- Optional tender: try stock 2-8-2 and 2-6-6-0; check all tender wheels turn,
  its clanks localise at the gear, chuffs at rear exhaust and cocks at cylinders.
- At rest, uncheck/reapply, re-enable/reapply, uncouple/reconnect, switch layouts,
  save/reload and delete. Check ordinary tender wheels and sound return cleanly.
- Check a second locomotive stays unchanged when reapplying one locomotive.

Build/managed/native-math checks do not replace in-game visual/audio acceptance.
Do not mix this trial with Realistic Firing for compatibility acceptance.
