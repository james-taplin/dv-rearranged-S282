The 1.4.2 capture also records simulation-update failure status and the originating
exception without suppressing it. This matters because a failed boiler tick can
skip engine torque/phase updates while native physics retains old torque. Record
from before a failure starts; an increasing RPM INPUT alone does not prove the
engine completed its tick. Compare per-engine crankPhase, chuffEvent and torque.
Samples include simUpdateFailed and simUpdateFault (type/message). The 10 Hz
capture samples faults rather than counting every exception or brief failure.
The new reported 0-8-0 run used infinite steam. Use ordinary steam/resources for
native validation. Earlier 400+ km/h reports had different unknowns; those logs
are retained as evidence, not a requirement to repeat RF-specific testing.

The file records car/arrangement, road speed and rolling RPM, live traction-port
wheel RPM, crank RPM, slip/slide, the reconstructed slip target, throttle/cutoff,
torque, force, pressures and steam flow. It records active mod versions too.
Wheel RPM means simulated traction RPM, not a measurement of the rendered wheel.
Normal smoothing and differently timed ECS updates can cause brief differences.
