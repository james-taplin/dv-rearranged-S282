Rearranged S282 B99 1.22.3 - geometry test candidate

This is a TEST build. It is not yet a Nexus release and has not been installed.
It supersedes the earlier uninstalled 1.22.3 guide-only candidate.

Changes
- Common motion frame and casing/guide alignment for resized rigid families,
  including twelve drivers. Existing driver locations and animation retained.
- Steam duct kit extends SatiricRug's original shaft profile, collars and style.
  Matched round elbows, reducers, compact risers and an offset Prairie casting.
- Original boiler branches retained where they fit. Six-coupled lower collar
  reduced only where its outer rim touched the relocated compressor outlet.
- Articulated front/middle branches mount on measured outboard casing faces;
  rear engines retain the original cast manifold. Horizontal headers stay clear
  of the reservoir/board. Erie tender supply remains internal.
- Original materials and texture regions reused. No new texture dependency.

Physics, boiler/steam consumption, accepted wheel proportions, cylinder motion,
Erie tender exhaust and stage sound tuning are retained. No new arrangements.

Offline validation
- Actual production builder reconstructed all 44 generated-pipe arrangements.
- Native body, tanks, steps and compressor meshes checked at both native LODs.
- All 44 neutral layouts and the synthetic +/-6-degree poses have no detected
  static triangle crossings. All 76 neutral/stress poses generate successfully.
- Larger synthetic +/-12-degree poses still have front engine/body contacts.
  These are a stress envelope with reconstructed pivots, not measured game
  curves. They are NOT certified clipping-free. In-game curve checks required.
- Source mesh/UV, winding, bore and adapter checks plus lifecycle/LOD ownership
  and protected compiled physics comparisons complement the surface audit.

Limits and manual checks
Triangle crossing tests do not prove minimum clearance, coplanar overlap,
nested solids or complete Franklin/main-rod motion. The test is not a Unity
rendering test. Check both sides in game, low and high speed, cutoff/reverse,
tight curves, near/far LOD, switching to stock, and a second locomotive.
Run the structural diagnostic once; it checks connections, not appearance.
Pay particular attention to the Prairie cast adapter's material finish and the
front pipe joints on Big Mallets/Franklin duplexes. Keep this off Nexus pending
that visual test. Existing 1.22.2 remains installed until installation is asked.
