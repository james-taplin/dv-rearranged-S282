2.0.9.3-beta - tender and Mallet repair test

Corrected final tender-clone mirror configuration. The exact newly added mirror
receives its copy-speed setting before activation; a disabled inherited mirror
awaiting Unity destruction can no longer receive that setting in its place.
This prevents the live mirror from replacing tender playback with a stopped
host Animator's speed.

Tender playback direction now reads the tender engine's own signed native RPM,
so independent front-engine slip or an opposed host RPM does not reverse it.
Inactive or missing tender simulation safely stops playback.

Reproduced a Walschaerts collision on the 2-6-6-2 Mallet (ID 37): its front
crank/main rod reached the following cylinder at -15 and +15 degrees of
articulation. A dedicated engine gap of 0.150 m replaces its former 0.100 m gap.
The complete rear engine and trailing axle move back 50 mm and the calculated
hinge moves back 25 mm. The 0.75 scale, native meshes and internal motion are
preserved; shared gaps for other layouts retain their values. Force calculations
and multiplayer transport are unchanged by these repairs.

Added an actual Unity regression using the game's native S282 controller and
all four driving wheels on both sides, through the production rear/template/
tender clone chain, deferred destruction and repeated detach/reapply. Native
asset playback and native engine calculation probes passed. Managed regressions
also cover source-cache rebuilding, frame clipping, effects/audio cleanup,
quartering, multiplayer policy and authority boundaries.

Strengthened structural diagnostics to capture local/effective settings and
inspect ownership, readiness and animation bindings. Static inventory and pose
checks cannot establish motion, live traction or multiplayer acceptance.

The final production-source sweep passed without a gap override: stock control
plus all 11 articulated layouts, 112,320 poses and 547 assertions, with no
unexpected contacts in the tested scope. ID 37 measured minimum separation of
42.308 mm at the crank bearing and 42.736 mm at the main rod. Sampled independent-
phase geometry grids also found no contacts; they do not exercise full-game
driving or independently slipping coupled vehicles.

The completed Franklin sweep compared 21,600 controller poses without
reproducing a new collision. Final old-gap and old ID 32 nose controls reproduced
1,200 and 648 contacts respectively, confirming detection of those known overlaps.
This does not establish
a universal clipping fix or clearance certificate. These scoped native probes
do not replace live coupled-vehicle driving or real host/guest acceptance.

No incompatible visual-settings combination has been confirmed. Preserve
Settings.xml and reapply changed preferences; online cars use the host's
effective profile. Reports should include both local and effective settings.

Retains 2.0.9.1-beta's arrangement-initialization/RPM-lease corrections and
optional-multiplayer startup diagnostics, the complete 49-arrangement catalogue,
experimental independent locomotive-group slip, radio requests and host locks.
Online players need the same complete package. Multiplayer is optional offline.
Powered-tender independent slip and true compound expansion remain unimplemented.
Live-game and real host/guest validation remain pending.
