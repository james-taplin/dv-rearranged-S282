PHYSICS NOTES

Existing effective driver-load adjustments translate intended adhesive weight
into DV's native friction calculation. Actual vehicle mass and native weather,
sanding, braking, curving and wheel-radius systems are retained.

Cylinder sizing is reduction-only against each engine's original specification.
The machinery-sensitive nominal FoA ceiling may fall above or below stock.
Stock is a reference, not a minimum FoA. Naturally weak original capacity is not
boosted. Rated mass is fixed for repeatable sizing when arrangements are selected;
actual native grip still follows current mass and rail conditions.

The 2-2-2 is excluded from calibration and practical balance conclusions.
Erie tender cylinders are calibrated separately; its simplified grip clamp remains.
One S282 boiler is retained. Nominal cylinder TE is not sustained power, drawbar
pull, top speed or a guaranteed tonnage rating. See FOA_BALANCE.md for calculations.
