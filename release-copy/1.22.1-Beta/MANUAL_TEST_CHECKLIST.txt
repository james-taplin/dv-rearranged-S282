OPTIONAL BETA TEST CHECKLIST

Confirm Running Rearranged S282 1.22.1.0 in UMM after a full game restart.
Check normal arrangement selection and save/reload on a familiar locomotive.
Compare stock with a small rigid and a larger rigid or Mallet under similar load.
Observe controlled starts, brief slip/recovery and ordinary steam consumption.
Check near/far gear and pipe appearance, oilcup refill and repeated switching.
For Erie, check tender coupling/detachment and treat it as experimental.

Diagnostic tools are optional for normal use. Structural test requires two
stationary S282s and cycles one through all arrangements; run in a suitable
sandbox. Power diagnostics creates timestamped files; stop/save after driving.
Report exact arrangement, version, conditions and screenshots/logs for defects.


ERIE VISUALS
Check the rear side-wall exhaust pipe bands and elbow from roof side and rear.
Open the water hatch and inspect clearances. Under power watch white steam
pulses at the pipe mouth; coast and check emission stops as pressure falls.
Open cylinder cocks and check tender jets on both sides. Drips require water
in the cylinders. Uncouple reconnect switch arrangement and save/reload.
